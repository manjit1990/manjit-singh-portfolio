01

Los Móviles: Conductor (Driver App)

A driver app on Google Play for Radio Taxis Libres S.A. in Bucaramanga, Colombia, with 10K+ downloads. Taxi drivers use it to go online, receive trips and reach passengers.

View on Google Play
My Role

Android Developer on the driver app.

Challenge

Drivers need to go online, accept a trip, reach the passenger on time and stay in contact while driving. The app has to be fast, easy to read and simple to use on the road.

Solution

A map-first home screen shows availability and service radius. Once a trip starts, one screen shows the pickup countdown, passenger details, payment method, pickup address and quick call and chat buttons.

Key Features

Availability status and service radius, live trip with pickup countdown, call and in-app chat, cash or taximeter payment info, notifications, adjustable text size, and a report option.

Android Google Play Live maps In-app chat

02

Daily Motivational Quotes & Affirmations

A motivational quotes and affirmations app that helps users find inspiration by mood, explore categories, save favorite quotes, create collections, and start each day with positive reminders.

View on Google Play ↗
My Role

Android development for the motivational quotes and affirmations experience, focusing on quote discovery by category and mood, favorites and collections, and a clear, engaging user interface.

Challenge

Users need to find quotes that fit their mood quickly, browse many categories without friction, and return to saved inspiration without searching for it again.

Solution

Structure the experience around searchable quote categories and mood-based discovery, with favorites and collections for saving meaningful quotes and daily reminders to encourage regular engagement.

Key Features

Quote categories, search, favorite quotes, collections, quote sharing, and daily motivational reminders.
