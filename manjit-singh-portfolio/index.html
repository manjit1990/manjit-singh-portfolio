AI Automation for Business

I build AI automation agents for your business.

Spend less time on repetitive tasks. Give your team more time for customers and growth.

An AI automation agent is software that understands a request, uses your business information, and takes approved actions across your tools. I build agents around your workflow so they can do more than answer questions: they can organize enquiries, update records, prepare follow-ups, and pass work to the right person.

Whether you run a service business, an online store, or a growing team, I can connect an agent to your website, mobile app, CRM, or internal systems, depending on the integrations available.

Discuss Your Automation Idea
Example workflow

From new enquiry to a prepared follow-up.

  1. A customer gets in touchThe agent captures their request and asks for any missing details.
  2. Your tools stay up to dateIt adds the lead to your CRM and routes the enquiry to the right team.
  3. Your team takes the next stepIt prepares a reply or booking suggestion for review, with the customer’s details ready.

Built around your rules, with human approval for sensitive actions and a handoff when the agent needs help.

Customer support

Answer common questions using your approved FAQs and product information, collect details, and hand complex requests to your team.

Leads & appointments

Qualify enquiries, capture contact details, check connected calendars, and help customers request appointments.

Documents & admin

Extract key details from documents, summarize messages, and prepare structured records for your team to review.

Daily operations

Connect routine tasks across your tools, send reminders, update task statuses, and prepare useful daily summaries.

Built for the way you work

We start with one time-consuming workflow and define what success looks like, such as faster responses or less manual data entry. I then build the integrations, test realistic scenarios with you, and help launch the agent with clear permissions, activity logs, and a path for human review. Results depend on your workflow, data quality, and connected tools.
