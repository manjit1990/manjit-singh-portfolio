AI Automation for Business

I build AI automation agents for your business.

Spend less time on repetitive tasks. Give your team more time for customers and growth.

An AI automation agent is software that understands a request, uses your business information, and takes approved actions across your tools. I build agents around your workflow so they can do more than answer questions: they can organize enquiries, update records, prepare follow-ups, and pass work to the right person.

Whether you run a service business, an online store, or a growing team, I can connect an agent to your website, mobile app, CRM, or internal systems, depending on the integrations available.

Los Móviles: Conductor (Driver App)

A driver app on Google Play for Radio Taxis Libres S.A. in Bucaramanga, Colombia, with 10K+ downloads. Taxi drivers use it to go online, receive trips and reach passengers.

View on Google Play
My Role

Android Developer on the driver app.

Challenge

Drivers need to go online, accept a trip, reach the passenger on time and stay in contact while driving. The app has to be fast, easy to read and simple to use on the road.

Solution

A map-first home screen shows availability and service radius. Once a trip starts, one screen shows the pickup countdown, passenger details, payment method, pickup address and quick call and chat buttons.

Key Features

Availability status and service radius, live trip with pickup countdown, call and in-app chat, cash or taximeter payment info, notifications, adjustable text size, and a report option.
